UpTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Request account deletion

This policy explains how Iulian Birladeanu (“Iulian”, “I”, or “we”) handles information when you use the UpTrack mobile app. UpTrack helps you track nutrition, workouts, body measurements, and activity. Iulian Birladeanu is the app developer and the contact for privacy requests.

Information handled

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, and name or profile image provided by your sign-in provider. UpTrack supports email and password, Google, and Apple sign-in.
  • Profile and fitness information: details you enter, such as age, height, weight, fitness goals and targets; weight entries; meal and food logs; workout history, plans, and custom exercises.
  • Steps: if you grant access to your device health service, UpTrack reads step information from Health Connect on Android or HealthKit on iOS and saves step summaries to your UpTrack account.
  • Food search and barcode information: food search terms and barcode numbers are sent to the food catalog service to find matching products. If you submit a barcode product, its barcode and product/nutrition details may be added to the shared catalog. Barcode images are processed for scanning and are not uploaded by the lookup flow.
  • On-device information: app preferences and temporary cached data are stored on your device to support app functionality.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure your account, save and display your profile and logs, calculate nutrition and fitness progress, retrieve food catalog results, and respond to support and deletion requests.

Service providers and sharing

UpTrack uses Google Firebase for account authentication and cloud storage of account/profile and app data, and Supabase to query and maintain shared food catalogs. Google or Apple process sign-in when you choose their sign-in option. These providers process information to provide their services to UpTrack. We do not sell personal information.

Barcode product details submitted by users are added to a shared catalog and are not linked to the submitter’s UpTrack account. Other users may receive those catalog entries in search results.

Health information and permissions

Step data is accessed through the device health service and its permissions. You can revoke access in your device settings. If you revoke it, UpTrack will no longer read new step data. Step summaries already saved to your UpTrack account can be removed by deleting your account.

Retention and deletion

Account information and account-linked fitness data are kept while your account is active. You can delete your account in UpTrack under Profile settings, or request deletion using the external method on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your Firebase account, account profile, account-owned records and profile picture; the app also clears account-scoped local data when available. Shared barcode product records are not associated with an account and are not removed when an account is deleted.

Health Connect and Apple Health data stored in those services is managed separately in those services. Deleting your UpTrack account removes the step summaries stored in your UpTrack account, not the original records in the device health service.

Your choices and rights

You can edit profile details in the app, revoke health permissions in device settings, and request account deletion. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also have rights to request access, correction, erasure, or restriction of personal data, object to certain processing, and lodge a complaint with your data protection supervisory authority. Contact us to make a privacy request.

Security

We use the security controls provided by our app platform and service providers and restrict account data access through authenticated app services. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version will be available at this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact iulian_birladeanu07@yahoo.com.
