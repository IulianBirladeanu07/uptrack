Delete your UpTrack account

Use this page to request deletion of your UpTrack account and the data associated with it. This request method is available even if you no longer have the app installed.

Email an account deletion request

What to include

Send the request from the email address associated with your UpTrack account and write “Delete my UpTrack account” in the message. Do not include your password. If you cannot send the request from that address, explain this in the email so we can verify the request safely.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the UpTrack account and associated account data, including profile information, meal logs, workout history and plans, measurements, custom exercises, and the profile picture stored by UpTrack. Account-scoped local data is cleared by the app when available.

Data that remains

Product and nutrition entries submitted to the shared barcode catalog are not linked to an UpTrack account and remain in that catalog after account deletion. Deletion of records stored in Health Connect or Apple Health is managed separately in those services; UpTrack deletes step summaries stored in your UpTrack account.

In-app deletion

If you can still sign in, you can also start deletion in UpTrack under Profile settings. The app may ask you to reauthenticate before completing deletion.
